Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy describes how Shamba Direct (“we”, “us”) handles personal information when you use our marketplace, create an account, list produce, place an order, or contact us. It applies to the website and related services.

Operator contact details: “Shamba Direct” is the service name. The registered legal operator name and complete business address have not yet been supplied. Before public launch, publish those details and a working support email. For privacy requests in the meantime, call 0718 031 596.

1. Information we handle

  • Account details: name, email address, account type, password hash, and whether the email address has been verified.
  • Farmer and business profiles: farm or business name, contact name, location, profile story, farming practices, and profile or cover photos.
  • Listings and content: product descriptions, prices, quantities, location, and uploaded product photos.
  • Orders and subscriptions: order items and totals, delivery name, telephone, area and address, fulfillment and payment status, provider transaction references, plan, and subscription dates.
  • Security and session information: session identifiers, authentication and verification tokens, and technical request information processed by our hosting and security providers.
  • Local browser storage: where demo or convenience features are used, the browser may store preferences or other page state on that device. Clearing browser storage removes locally saved data. Do not rely on a shared device to keep such information private.

2. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify email addresses, display farmer profiles and listings, route and manage orders, check subscription status, process or verify payments, provide customer support, prevent fraud and abuse, maintain the service, and meet legal obligations. We use uploaded photos to display the profile or listing for which you supplied them. Public listings and profile information may be visible to other users and visitors.

3. Who may receive information

We share only information needed to operate the service, handle a transaction, or meet a legal obligation. Depending on which services are configured, recipients may include:

  • Farmers or sellers, with the order and delivery details needed to fulfill a buyer’s order; buyers, with seller profile and listing information.
  • Hosting and database providers, including Render and Netlify, to operate the website, API, and stored account/order records.
  • Cloudflare R2 for uploaded profile and listing photos. Published marketplace photos may be publicly accessible by their image URL.
  • Resend for account verification and password-reset emails.
  • Stripe for card payments and recurring subscriptions, and Safaricom/Daraja for M-Pesa payment prompts and verification, when those methods are enabled.
  • Authorities or other parties when disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, rights, or service security.

Payment providers process payment information under their own privacy notices. Shamba Direct does not intend to store full card numbers or card security codes.

4. Cookies and similar storage

The live service uses an HTTP-only session cookie to keep you signed in and a session security token to protect account actions. These are needed for core functionality. Browser-local storage may also be used for page preferences or demo state. The service does not currently describe or rely on third-party advertising cookies; if that changes, this policy and any required consent controls will be updated.

5. Retention and security

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain account and transaction records, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, comply with legal requirements, and protect the service. When information is no longer needed, we will delete it or remove identifying details where reasonably practicable, subject to legal, security, and backup limitations. We use access controls, password hashing, protected sessions, and encrypted connections where supported by the deployed providers. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Transfers and processing locations

Our service providers may store or process information in countries outside Kenya. Where cross-border processing occurs, we will use arrangements and safeguards required by applicable data-protection law. Provider availability and processing locations depend on the services selected by the operator.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to personal information about you, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, or other available rights. You can update some profile information through your account. To make a privacy request or report a concern, call 0718 031 596. A request may require reasonable identity verification to protect your account. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

You may request account and associated personal-data deletion through the same contact. Some records may need to be retained for completed orders, payment reconciliation, fraud prevention, legal obligations, or resolving disputes; we will restrict them to those purposes where required.

8. Children

The marketplace is intended for adults conducting buying or selling activities and is not directed to children. Do not create an account if you are not legally able to enter the relevant agreement. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our service, providers, or legal obligations change. We will change the date above and provide notice or request consent when required by law. Please review this page periodically.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, access/correction/deletion requests, or complaints, call 0718 031 596. A support email, registered legal operator name, and full business address must be published here before public launch. You may also contact the relevant Kenyan data-protection authority where applicable.
